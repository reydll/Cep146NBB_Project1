# How Cybersecurity Works
## Firewalls
Firewalls are walls between you and the internet. It keeps track of everything that comes in and out. Protecting your computer from malware, ransomware, and phishing. 

## Encryption
Is converting sensitive information into unreadable code. To access the information you need a key to  decode it. This makes it harder for others to get your sensitive information. This includes information like emails, financial transactions and cloud storage.

## Multi-Factor Authentication(MFA)
Multi-factor authentication is second verification. Instead of just typing in your password it sends a one time message through either your email or phone number. Asking to type in the code to double check that it's you. This adds a second layer of protection. 

## Antivirus Software
Antivirus Software scans your devices for dangerous threats like viruses, worms, and Trojans. Getting rid of them before anything damage is caused. Acting like a shield protecting anything that comes inside.
