# **CYBERSECURITY**

Nowadays , Everyone is using IT technologies for everything , not only for watching videos or playing , but more importantly to store their notes from work or study , their informations but even their banking data. So for protecting those data , we use what we call ***Cybersecurity***. 
## **1.What really is *Cybersecurity* ?**
***Cybersecurity*** is the practice of protecting systems , networks, and program from digital attacks by using various technologies , processes and policies. These ***Cyberattacks**** are usually aimed at accessing , changing , or destroying sensitive information , for instance , extorting money from users through ***ransomware**** ; or interrupting normal processes. 

***Cybersecurity*** is ,then, a *key* to overall ***cyber risk management*** strategy. Common cybersecurity threats include ***ransomware**** and other ***malware**** , ***phishing**** scams , ***data theft**** and more recently , attacks powered by ***artificial intelligence**** (AI). 

### Don't really get what ***Cyberattacks*** is ?
A ***Cyberattacks*** is an attempt by an individual or group that we often call ***Hackers*** to breach , damage ,or disrupt computer systems ,networks ,or digital devices , often for malicious purposes such as data theft , espionage ,financial fraud ,or system sabotage.

Just think about cyberattacks  like if someone just get all of your money in your bank account without you noticing , pretty bad right? , So we need Cybersecurity mainly for protecting everyone's data from hackers .


# Wanna_cry ransomware attack 
 
## What is Wanna_cry ransomware attack and how did it work? 
 Wanna_cry was malware, not virus.  It was based of Eternal blue virus that was made by NSA (National Security Agency). The only difference was that it added on a patch that is supposed to bring pop up where it asks bitcoin as well as added checker to see whenether it was being tested in sandbox. On monitors of over 100,000 computers who still had old Windows  Wanna_cry targeted them and the users of the computer received a pop up. It was timer with condition that if person who received the malware won't send bitcoin to certain address then all their personal information will be leaked. Finds out later Wanna_cry was made by Lazarus group who are sponsored hackers by North Korea who often hacked South Korea people. 
**How did it stop?** 
The way Wanna_cry ransomware attack was finally stopped was due to that hackers created a secure way to make sure their hack wouldn't be able to test. It took requests from fake empty domain and if domain responded with nothing then the malware would continue the work of getting in computer, but if it got responses of domain responding then the malware would stop the work because it would immediately understand its in special sandbox that is supposed to check and see how malware acts. To stop the malware, Marcus Hutchins bought the domain Wanna_cry was working on which led to domain respond positive no  matter what computer it was on, even outside the sandbox tester because the program thought it was stil being tested.  
### "Eternal Blue"
Eternal Blue was hacking method NSA created to spy on different countries government  to hack their system and see all their files. Windows had vulnerability of it not being set to keep files until certain size which after that it would start going out of 'the boundary' where it is kept under secure, safe place. The reason why Windows didn't see it before themselves was due it being unseen. In 2017, the NSA was hacked by unknown people who got the files of Eternal Blue and how it works. At first they tried to sell it, but it was failed and then tried to manipulate government, but also left without answer due to everything being written in broken English. In the end they posted it for free access in dark web, but only after a month of Windows making update where they patch the vulnerability making it not exist after .
